# Goodwill Credit

When a customer is outside the return window but has had a genuinely poor
experience, an agent may offer goodwill store credit instead of a refund.
Goodwill credit is capped at 20 percent of the order total and is issued as store
credit, never as a refund to a card.

Goodwill credit is a judgement call, not an entitlement, and it does not extend
the return window. It should be offered at most once per customer per order.

If the customer declines store credit and continues to ask for a cash refund on
an order outside the window, the correct next step is to explain the policy once
more and offer to escalate to a human specialist.
