# Delayed and Lost Parcels

A domestic parcel is considered delayed once it is more than three business days
past its estimated delivery date, and lost once it is ten business days past.
International parcels are considered lost at thirty business days.

For a delayed parcel, ask the customer to check the tracking link and confirm the
delivery address on the order. Carriers frequently update tracking late, and a
parcel marked delayed often arrives without further action.

For a parcel confirmed lost, the customer is entitled to a replacement shipment
at no cost, or a full refund if the item is no longer in stock. A lost-parcel
claim is not a standard return and is not limited by the 30-day return window.
