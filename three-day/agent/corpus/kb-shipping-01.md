# Shipping Times and Tracking

Standard domestic shipping arrives in three to five business days. Expedited
shipping arrives in one to two business days. International shipping takes seven
to twenty-one business days depending on the destination and customs handling.

Every shipped order has a tracking number, which is emailed to the customer when
the carrier scans the parcel. Agents can read the current status and tracking
number for an order from the order record.

An order that has not been scanned by the carrier will show as processing rather
than shipped. Processing normally lasts under one business day.
