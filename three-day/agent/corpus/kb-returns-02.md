# Exceptions to the Return Window

A small number of categories fall outside the standard 30-day return window.

Final-sale and clearance items cannot be returned or exchanged at any point.
Perishable goods, opened personal-care products, and gift cards are also
non-returnable. Made-to-order and personalised items cannot be returned unless
the item arrived damaged or the personalisation does not match the order.

Items that arrive damaged or defective may be returned at any time within the
warranty period regardless of the 30-day window. In that case the return is
handled as a warranty claim rather than a standard return, and the customer
should be asked to describe the damage before any refund is discussed.
