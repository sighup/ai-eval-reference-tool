# How Refunds Are Processed

Once a return is approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method.
Card refunds usually appear within five to seven business days, though the exact
timing depends on the customer's bank. Refunds to store credit are immediate.

A refund is normally limited to the amount the customer actually paid for the
order. The refundable amount is the order total, less any shipping charges that
have already been used, and less the value of any promotional discount applied at
checkout.

Partial refunds are available when only some items in a multi-item order are
being returned. In that case the refund covers the returned items only. Agents
should confirm the order total before discussing any specific refund amount.
