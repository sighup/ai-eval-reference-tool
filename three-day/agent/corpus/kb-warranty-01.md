# Warranty Coverage

Northwind Goods covers manufacturing defects for twelve months from the order
date. Coverage includes materials failure, seam and joint separation, and
hardware that fails under normal use.

Warranty coverage does not extend to accidental damage, ordinary wear, or damage
caused by use the product was not designed for. Consumable parts such as filters
and batteries are excluded.

A warranty claim needs the order number and a description of the fault. Approved
claims are resolved with a repair, a replacement, or a refund of the item price,
in that order of preference. Warranty claims are handled by a human specialist,
so an agent should escalate rather than resolve them directly.
