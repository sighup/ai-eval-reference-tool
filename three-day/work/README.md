# Your work

Everything you make in the three days goes here, and is worth committing:

- `labels.csv`: your pass/fail labels, written by `lab/label.py` (Day 2)
- `criteria.md`: your failure modes, who owns each, and each judge's measured
  TPR and TNR (Days 2 and 3)
- `judges/*.md`: your judge prompts (Day 3)

`results/` holds grader output and the judge cache. It is regenerated by the
tools and ignored by git.
