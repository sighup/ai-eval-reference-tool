# Your work

Everything you make in the three days goes here, and is worth committing:

- `labels.csv`: your pass/fail labels, written by `lab/label.py` (dev on Day 2,
  test on Day 3)
- `criteria.md`: your failure modes, who owns each, your decisions on false
  alarms, and each judge's measured TPR and TNR (Days 2 and 3)
- `judges/*.md`: your judge prompts (Day 3)

`results/` holds grader output, the judge cache and the climb log
(`climb-log.csv`). It is written by the tools and ignored by git.
